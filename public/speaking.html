Speaking

Book Zolile to speak

Zolile Nomaqhiza CA(SA) failed six times on the road to qualifying. He speaks about what kept him getting up: discipline, faith, resilience and the long road to the finish line. Every talk is shaped around your audience, from first-year students to boardrooms.

Popular topics

Each talk draws on Zolile’s 16-year journey to CA(SA) and is tailored to the people in the room.

Keynote

Fall Down Seven Times, Get Up Eight

Six failures, one designation. An honest account of setback after setback, and the decisions that turned a dark valley into a finish line.

Practical

Discipline Is the DNA of Success

Why motivation fades and systems hold. The four daily practices of stillness, movement, reading and writing, and how to keep them small enough to survive difficult days.

Students & trainees

The Valley Is Not a Permanent Residence

For university students, trainees and IAC/APC candidates: exam strategy, the “Red Pen Method”, handling results day, and separating “I failed” from “I am a failure.”

Teams & leaders

Failure Is Information, Not Identity

Building resilient people and teams: how setbacks become data for redesign, and why devotion outlasts performance.
