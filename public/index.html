The Testimony Behind The Movement

There are thousands of people in South Africa right now who are in the valley, wondering if they should keep going. This brand was built for them.

Disciplined Disciples was founded by Zolile Nomaqhiza, a qualified CA(SA), whose journey included six unsuccessful board attempts before finally qualifying on his seventh. This movement exists to prove that your current chapter is not your final chapter.

Every book page, mentorship conversation, and garment is designed to help someone carry on when quitting feels easier. We are building a tribe for everyone the valley has not finished yet.
