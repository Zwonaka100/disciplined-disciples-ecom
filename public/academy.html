Flagship programme

SAICA APC Programme

Our core mission is to equip candidates with the exact competencies needed to excel in the SAICA APC board examination. We don’t just teach you how to study; we train you to think, write and act like a seasoned professional advisor.

Professional Writing Framework

Our comprehensive 33-page master guide on how to structure, articulate and write like a high-level professional advisor. Available exclusively to programme candidates.

Diagnostic + 5 one-on-one sessions

We begin with a diagnostic deep-dive to find where you stand in your APC journey, followed by five live one-on-one sessions. Strictly non-recorded: personal, human and tailored to you.

Which package fits you?

Use this quick guide to choose confidently, whether you are a student, a board-exam candidate, a trainee, a professional, a founder or in a career transition.

Academy FAQs

Is this the same as the old Mentorship?

Yes. Mentorship is now called the Disciplined Disciples Academy. Existing packages, sessions and payments are unchanged, and paid packages now include the 30-Day Journal.

Is this only for CA students?

No. We support the whole CA journey (university, IAC, training contract, APC) and also mentor professionals in other fields, founders and career changers.

How quickly will I hear back?

Discovery requests and APC applications are reviewed within 5-7 business days. Paid packages are confirmed as soon as PayFast confirms your payment.

Is the AI toolkit allowed in the exam?

The AI toolkit is for preparation and research before the exam: understanding triggers, industries and ratios. It is not used in the exam room.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes. Sessions can be moved with at least 24 hours notice so your slot can be managed fairly.

What if I am still unsure?

Start with Discovery. It is designed to help you choose the right next step without pressure.
