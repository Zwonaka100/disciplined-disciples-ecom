About This Book

In 2009, Zolile Nomaqhiza walked into Wits University and missed the cut for BAccSci by just 2 points. What followed was 16 years of exams, setbacks, severe eczema, grief, a gap year he called surrender — and finally, in 2026, his CA(SA) designation.

This is not a book about success. It is a book about the spaces between attempts. About what happens when you have no wins to show and no guarantee that the next try will be different. About faith when the numbers don't add up. About discipline when motivation has long since gone.

Relentlessly Disciplined is for everyone who has ever felt like they are the only person in the room still waiting for their breakthrough.
