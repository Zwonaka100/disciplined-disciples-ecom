Mentorship FAQs

How quickly will I hear back?

Discovery requests are reviewed within 5-7 business days. Paid packages are confirmed immediately after successful checkout.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes. Sessions can be moved with at least 24 hours notice so your slot can be managed fairly.

Is this only for CA students?

No. It is open to learners, students, professionals at all levels, founders, and career pivoters. The focus is disciplined progress, regardless of your field.

What if I am still unsure?

Start with Discovery. It is designed to help you choose the right next step without pressure.
