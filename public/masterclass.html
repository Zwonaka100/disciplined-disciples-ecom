Questions

Do I need a Microsoft account or the Teams app?

No. The join link opens in the Teams app if you have it, or in your browser if you don’t. Join from a laptop if you can, so you can follow the paper.

Why do I join with my ticket number?

Everyone waits in the Teams lobby and is let in by name. Entering your name and ticket number (for example “Thandi Mokoena DD-1004”) lets us admit you quickly. Your ticket email shows exactly what to type.

I can’t make it live. Can I still buy a ticket?

Yes. Every ticket holder is emailed the recording after the session.

I paid but haven’t received my ticket.

Tickets usually arrive within a few minutes. Check your spam or promotions folder for an email from Zolile Nomaqhiza. Still nothing after 15 minutes? WhatsApp +27 69 206 0618 and we’ll resend it.

Can I get a refund?

Tickets are non-refundable, but you’ll still receive the recording if you can’t attend.
